# Hope Agent 0.44.0 — Faster custom model setup

> [简体中文](https://github.com/shiwenwen/hope-agent/blob/v0.44.0/docs/release-notes/v0.44.0.md) · **English**

> Released: 2026-09-06

> See [the changelog](https://github.com/shiwenwen/hope-agent/blob/v0.44.0/CHANGELOG.md#0440---2026-09-06) for the full list.

When adding or editing a custom model, enter a model name, model ID, or provider name to search the built-in catalog. Selecting a suggestion fills in capabilities such as context length, image input, and reasoning, reducing manual lookup and entry. You can still adjust these yourself.

Your current provider connection settings are preserved. If catalog pricing differs from your entered prices and uses the same currency, you can review the source and unit prices before choosing to keep your prices or use the catalog prices. Prices in another currency never directly overwrite yours.

Suggestions come from the built-in catalog and do not guarantee that your provider offers the model. When using a relay, check the suggestion's source and adjust the model ID, capabilities, and pricing to match what your provider actually supports.
