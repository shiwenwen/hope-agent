# Hope Agent v0.72.0

## More model choices

When adding a provider in Settings, you can select GPT-6.1 Terra, Kimi K3.1 Pro and its Highspeed variant, Unbiased Pareto 26.10, and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash on Volcengine Ark. API and Codex subscription access, context, and costs remain separate, and saved configurations are preserved. Choose according to your account access and costs; new presets do not establish real-account acceptance. See [Models and providers](https://github.com/shiwenwen/hope-agent/blob/v0.72.0/docs/user-guide/en/02-models-and-providers.md).

## More accurate task and design checks

After you revise a goal, verified repairs remain resolved, while the revised goal still needs fresh acceptance evidence. Design Space explicitly reports unsupported custom visual scenarios as incomplete and prevents accepting default-page screenshots as their baseline. Desktop, tablet, and phone checks remain available for the default scenario.

## Reply and compatibility fixes

Google Chat previews and final replies retain their original text format, reducing changes to links and mentions. Perplexity search filters duplicate or invalid citations and no longer records failures or cancellations as successful searches; existing search settings are preserved. Encrypted backups and WeChat media retain their existing formats, and the Stepfun icon adapts to the updated theme display.

Specialized evaluations correct fault grading, stop states, and call-usage attribution. These repairs do not establish model capability or real-device regression acceptance.
