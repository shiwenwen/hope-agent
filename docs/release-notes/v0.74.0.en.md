# Hope Agent v0.74.0

## Give your assistant a name and avatar

The assistant's default name is Hope. Click the avatar or settings icon in the conversation header, then open **Assistant settings → Profile** to rename it, upload and crop an image, choose a preset, or restore the default avatar. Saving updates the sidebar tooltip, conversation header, and greeting while preserving the conversation, history, delegated work, and permissions.

## Hide Items and keep talking

Delegated work and followed goals now live in **Items**. Expand or collapse the right panel on wide windows, or open a dialog on smaller windows to leave more room for the conversation. You can still open the original work conversation when you need to take over.

## Connect Telegram in Assistant settings

Bot connection, owner pairing, conversation permissions, and notifications now live in **Assistant settings → IM connection**, keeping connection forms out of the conversation. Existing work bots keep their original IM settings.

Telegram manages the Bot name and avatar separately; changing the assistant's HA profile does not update its Telegram profile. If you use a standalone Server, update both the desktop and Server to this version.

## A slimmer sidebar with full workbench controls

The global sidebar is narrower and uses icons with hover labels. The assistant avatar stays pinned at the top instead of moving into **More** on shorter windows. Assistant conversations retain workbench tab switching, closing, floating, and maximizing controls, and you can reopen the same workbench after opening **Items**.

See the [chat and sessions guide](https://github.com/shiwenwen/hope-agent/blob/v0.74.0/docs/user-guide/en/03-chat-and-sessions.md) and [full changelog](https://github.com/shiwenwen/hope-agent/blob/v0.74.0/CHANGELOG.md).
